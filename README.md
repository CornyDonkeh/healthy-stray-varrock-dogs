# Healthy Stray Dogs
A plugin that causes the stray dogs and Duke, Charlie the Tramp's dog, to appear healthy using the healthy model of the doggo that sits outside of the clan hall. The clan hall doggo was chosen as it most closely matches the strays, except without fleas or a gaping wound in its side. 

##Features 
- Dogs will have gone to the vet to have the gaping wound on their ribs treated
- Doggos will have had proper outpatient care, including baths, time to regrow the fur on their ribs
- Doggos have had baths
- Doggos have been given anti-flea, tick, and heartworm treatment
- Notice: All changes are cosmetic
